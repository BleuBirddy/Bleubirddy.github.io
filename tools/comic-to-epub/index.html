Comic to EPUB

Turn CBZ, CBR and PDF comics into EPUBs for your phone, tablet or e-reader, with foggy speech-bubble lettering cleaned up so it's easier to read.

Options

    Private by design

    Everything happens in your browser. Your comics are never uploaded: this page has no server, and it keeps working offline once loaded. Big issues can take a minute or two, especially on a phone.

    What the clean-up does

    It finds lettering on white speech bubbles and captions and redraws the ink solid and crisp on flat paper, so it stays readable when shrunk to a small screen. Pages without changes are copied exactly as they were. White-on-black captions, colored caption boxes and sound effects are left as drawn.

    Made by Bleubirddy. Uses JSZip, libarchive.js and pdf.js.
