Small tools and apps for reading, watching and making things.

The tools run in your browser and the apps run on your device. Your files are never uploaded.

Tools

  • Comic to EPUB

    Turn CBZ, CBR and PDF comics into EPUBs, with foggy speech-bubble lettering cleaned up.

  • Comic Yassifier

    Get comics ready for the Xteink X3: bigger speech bubbles, smart page splits, crisp 4-shade output.

  • Sleep Screen Maker

    Turn any image into a sleep screen for Xteink, Supernote, Kobo and Kindle e-readers.

Apps

  • Koios iPhone and iPad · in beta

    A reader for the comics, ebooks, PDFs and audiobooks you own. No ads, no account, no tracking. Privacy policy

  • RePaint iPhone, iPad and Mac

    A slicer for 3D printing, plus a painter that turns a picture into a filament painting.

  • ReWatch iPhone, iPad and Mac

    A media player built on mpv. It plays mkv, flac and other formats Apple's player won't open.

  • PhoneZip iPhone

    Copies your photos, videos and documents to a USB drive and checks every file.
