Bleubirddy is a hobby site for the tools and apps I make.

Most of them are about reading: getting comics and books onto e-readers and phones, and making them easier to read once they are there.

Your files stay yours

The tools on this site run in your browser. There is no server behind them, so your comics and images are never uploaded, and there is no account, no ads and no tracking.

One thing to know: Sleep Screen Maker loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google sees that request. Your image still never leaves your device.

Code and contact

The code for this site and for most of the apps is on GitHub. If a tool breaks or gets a comic wrong, please open an issue there.
