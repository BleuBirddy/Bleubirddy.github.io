Koios iPhone and iPad · in beta

A reader for the comics, ebooks, PDFs and audiobooks you already own. You bring your own DRM-free files; there is no store and no server.

  • Reads: CBR, CBZ, EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, PDF, saved web pages (HTML), MP3, M4B and M4A.
  • Library: covers, series, reading status, search, sort and filter.
  • Marks: highlights with notes in ebooks and book PDFs, and bookmarks in everything.
  • Audiobooks: lock screen controls and a sleep timer.
  • Backups: an export carries your books and your reading data, and importing it restores the library.

Koios is in beta testing and its code is not public yet. Privacy policy

RePaint iPhone, iPad and Mac

Two tools for 3D printing in one app.

  • Painter: turns a picture into a filament painting, a thin flat print where the darker layers show through the thin areas. You choose the filaments, and it exports an STL and a plan of the layer where each filament starts.
  • Slicer: opens an STL, shows it on the print bed, slices it into layers and saves the G-code.

ReWatch iPhone, iPad and Mac

A media player built on mpv. It plays the files you already have, where they already are, including formats Apple's own player won't open.

  • Plays: mkv, mp4, avi, webm, flac, mp3, opus and many more, with DTS and Dolby TrueHD sound.
  • Tracks: switch between a file's audio and subtitle tracks with one tap.
  • Resume: closing a file saves your place.
  • Offline: import a file to keep a copy on the device, so it plays with the drive unplugged.

ReWatch on GitHub

PhoneZip iPhone

Copies your photos, videos and documents onto a USB drive, on their way to a NAS or anywhere else you keep backups.

  • Whole library: photos, videos, Live Photos, screenshots and the folders you pick from Files.
  • Checked: every file is checked after it lands on the drive.
  • Bigger than the phone: it works in zipped parts, so a library larger than the phone's free space still goes out in one run.
  • Your call: it deletes the originals from the phone only if you say so.
